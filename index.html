How It Works

Compounds every cycle (4 cycles/day — 120/month or 1,460/year). Each period builds on the previous period's Token Closing (after that period's withdrawal). From your configured start period, Withdraw % of the tokens added each period is withdrawn, converted to USD net of Sales Tax, then to INR.

A = P × (1 + r)n
A = Final amount P = Principal (opening balance) r = Rate per cycle (decimal) n = Cycles this period
